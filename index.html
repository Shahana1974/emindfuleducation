Frequently Asked Questions

Questions? We Have Answers.

Who can enrol in these courses?

Students in Classes 9 and 10 following ICSE or CBSE can enquire about suitable programmes.

Are classes conducted online?

Yes. The programmes are designed for online learning. Class platform and timings are shared during enrolment.

What is the difference between tuition and a crash course?

Regular tuition provides ongoing concept development and practice. Crash courses focus on revision, important topics and exam preparation.

Do students need a robotics kit?

Some activities can be completed using virtual tools. Any additional equipment requirements will be communicated for the selected programme.

How can I enquire about fees and batches?
